## Decisions

- T-0174 diagnosed from the sources instead of a live deploy: signet's `holdLock` selects only on `ctx.Done()` and heartbeats, so a half-close never ends the stream, and signet-client 0.9.0's `Lock.release()` awaits `recvDone` forever. I-0031's guess (app.close() waits on keep-alive connections) does not hold: Fastify 5 closes idle connections by default, and a SIGTERM in the window exits in about 1s through the already settled `closeOnce()`. Recorded upstream as I-0039; signet and signet-client are other repositories, so they are not changed here.
- The fix bounds `lock.release()` at 2s and lets the process exit: the exit closes the gRPC connection, the RPC's context is cancelled, and `holdLock`'s deferred Release frees the lock at once. No grace on `app.close()` and no final exit deadline, since nothing shows either step hanging (code-quality: fix root causes, no speculative special cases).
- T-0179 cancelled: chronicle's shutdown exits before `lock.release()`, so it never hangs, and the exit frees the lock. Portcullis is not changed: the Rust client breaks its loop after `close_send()` and drops the stream.
- Layers: T-0175 (tower) alone, since T-0176 (herald) and T-0177 (keep) copy its module and spec; T-0176 and T-0177 run together, with no path in common; T-0181 waits for all three fixes, since it measures them live; T-0184 waits for T-0181, whose figures its reason quotes.
- Shared design/issues/summary.md with S-0007 (MS-0095) and S-0010 (MS-0097), and keep/TESTING.md plus summary.md with S-0008 (MS-0096): S-0038 changes summary.md only through flai's regeneration, and keep/TESTING.md only in its restart paragraph.
- Criterion 2 is ticked on the live run for tower and herald (exit 0, 2s after the lock, no liveness kill) and on the behavior spec for keep, whose bundle did not change during the run but which runs the same module.
- The checks of s-0038 failed only at golden-path step 5 with herald's OAEP decoding error. That is I-0027's rotation race (D-0052), so I bumped I-0027 rather than changing this branch. Tower's extra ~10s down after its second quick exit is the kubelet's restart back-off, recorded as I-0041 (impression).
