## Decisions

- Proposed fix for I-0035, as the Goal asks: the golden path takes a restart marker (pod names and container restart counts) of each workload whose signet bundle a step changes, and after the step waits for that workload's signet-coordinated restart to finish (marker changed and every container Ready) before the next step. A restart counts as begun when the marker changes, a container stops being Ready, or the workload logs its restart-lock line after the step began. With none begun within a 45s settle window (SIGNET_RESTART_SETTLE_SECONDS) it goes on, so a rerun that changes no bundle passes; once begun it waits up to 120s (SIGNET_RESTART_TIMEOUT_SECONDS) and dies naming the workload otherwise. Step 1 waits for tower and portcullis (tower/src/routes/provisioning.ts pushes to both); step 5 waits for herald and tower after the rotation and before the invite (herald/src/routes/operator-rotate-keys.ts pushes to both), which also makes tower encrypt the invite with the rotated key (I-0030's second instance).
- Kept the planner's plan (TH-0075): layer 1 T-0178 (lib.sh helper); layer 2 T-0180 (step 1 waits; after T-0178, whose helper it calls); layer 3 T-0182 (step 5 waits; after T-0180, same script and its port-forward check); layer 4 T-0183 (README) and T-0185 (kluster runs, close I-0035) together, both after T-0182 and sharing no path.
- Worked T-0178, T-0180, and T-0182 myself rather than through task sub-agents: each is a few dozen lines of shell in two files already read here, so a sub-agent's brief would cost more than the edit.
- Added NotReady as a third begin signal beside the planner's marker and log line: tower's tcpSocket readiness probe marks it NotReady about 15s into its restart-lock hang, before its restart count rises.
- The first deploy of s-0039 was interrupted mid-rollout (07:49:28Z) when S-0022's review deploy started. The redeploy's signet step reseeded the bundles of services already running, so tower, keep, herald, and chronicle restarted after the deploy passed; the first checks run's step 1 hit tower's closed port (curl 35, no FAIL line: I-0032). Recorded as I-0040.
- Fixed I-0040 here, as new task T-0186 (touches scripts/kluster-deploy.sh, integration/scripts/lib.sh, integration/README.md), rather than tearing s-0039 down: a teardown would leave S-0014 and S-0022 holding both namespace slots, and every deploy at the branch's head reseeds signet, so no checks run at the head could pass without it. T-0185 now waits for T-0186. The deploy marks tower, keep, herald, chronicle, and portcullis before the signet step and waits for their restarts after the rollouts, through lib.sh's helper, which now takes the deploy's kube context (KLUSTER_KUBE_CONTEXT).
